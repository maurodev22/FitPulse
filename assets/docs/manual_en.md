# FitPulse Manual

FitPulse is your performance and wellness companion. All your data is stored
**only on your device**: no internet connection and no accounts required.

## Getting started

1. Accept the **Terms and Conditions** to continue.
2. Create your **athletic profile**: full name, age, sex, weight, height and main
   goal. BMI is estimated live as you enter weight and height.
3. Tap **"Save and Enter Dashboard"** to begin.

## Main tabs

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Greeting, "Perfect Day" card with your 3 daily goals, steps/calories/pulse summary, recommended workout and categories. |
| **Recipes** | Search recipes by text, filter by category, browse the catalog and log calories and macros to your daily balance. |
| **Progress** | Body weight and BMI, active burn, weekly chart, recent sessions and badges. |
| **Tips** | Personalized plan based on your goal, high-impact tips, articles and community. |
| **Profile** | Edit your data, preferences, training days or change the language. |
| **Help** | This manual and frequently asked questions. |

## Today's balance

In **Recipes**, tapping **"Log to my balance"** adds today's calories, protein,
carbs and fats. The balance accumulates while the app is open and is kept
between sessions.

## How to change the language

1. Open the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap the language selector (Español/English).
3. The app switches instantly and remembers your choice.

## Important notice

professional health advice. Always consult a health professional.
